Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023)
Obligations of Data Fiduciaries as enacted. Version 1.0 = the Act; version 2.0 adds the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.
Source: https://www.meity.gov.in (operative requirements reproduced in summary form)

5.1 Every request for consent shall be accompanied or preceded by a notice in clear and plain language informing the Data Principal of the personal data and the purpose for which it is proposed to be processed.
6.1 The consent given by the Data Principal shall be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous with a clear affirmative action, and limited to the personal data necessary for the specified purpose.
6.4 The Data Fiduciary shall allow the Data Principal to withdraw consent at any time, with the ease of doing so comparable to the ease with which consent was given.
8.5 A Data Fiduciary shall protect personal data in its possession or under its control by taking reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breach.
8.6 In the event of a personal data breach, the Data Fiduciary shall give intimation of such breach to the Data Protection Board and to each affected Data Principal.
8.7 A Data Fiduciary shall erase personal data upon the Data Principal withdrawing consent or as soon as it is reasonable to assume that the specified purpose is no longer being served.
8.10 A Data Fiduciary shall establish an effective mechanism to redress the grievances of Data Principals.
9.1 Before processing any personal data of a child, a Data Fiduciary shall obtain the verifiable consent of the parent or lawful guardian.
